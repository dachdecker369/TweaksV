# Roadmap

What I plan for TweaksV next, before it is planned in [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) and specified in [SPECIFICATIONS.md](SPECIFICATIONS.md). This file is new with 2.0.0; nothing stood here before 2.1.0, because TweaksV did what I wanted it to do. It held two versions once, 2.2.0 and 2.2.1, both released on 2026-10-25 from a player's report - with NaturalVision Enhanced installed, TweaksV crashed the game; the findings and the design are in NOTES.md and SPECIFICATIONS.md now. Since 2026-10-26 it held six: a limit of the DirectStorage part to lift, not urgent, and five new parts, one adjuster each, towards TweaksV as the one place for tweaking GTA V Enhanced and adjusting its limits. The first of the new parts, Blips, came with 2.3.0 on 2026-10-28, and the DirectStorage limit was lifted with 2.4.0; 2.5.0 measured the other four parts' seven limits first, and from its numbers and my second test's (PLAN.md, D10 to D18) WeaponLimits, PedProps and Resources came with 2.6.0, PedProps experimental and off by default until a small clean pack of one prop has shown that its raise works past 255 (NOTES.md, T-PEDPROPS) - together with a part and a check that came up on the way: RayTracing (the ray tracing buffer of moving geometry) and RageOpenV's check for packs made for GTA V Legacy - while the streaming heap, which never ran short, was dropped (NOTES.md, Versions). StaticBounds, the Static Bounds Limit Adjuster rewritten as TweaksV's own, came up on the way as well and left again before 2.6.0's tag (PLAN.md, D15). So it holds two now, phase C's: SirenSettings and StaticBounds, as 2.7.0. A new part is a part like the thirteen (SPECIFICATIONS.md, section 16): its own file on the shared code - the scanner, the patch module, the relocation core, the three tables - its own section of `TweaksV.ini`, its old plugin in the conflict check, its measurement under `DIAGNOSTIC_MODE`, its suite, its patterns in PATTERNS.md. One minor version per part, a patch version for its fixes, but parts built in one round ship together as one version, as 2.6.0's did (PLAN.md, D18); a version takes the next free number when it ships. Two plugins made for Enhanced join the conflict check once their files are known (PLAN.md, D9): WeaponLimitsAdjuster Enhanced, whose page awaits approval, and the withdrawn Resource Adjuster Enhanced.

How a part is taken over depends on the original's license, as [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md) says of the thirteen. Code under a license the GPL-3.0 takes in - MIT, BSD, zlib, the GPL-3.0 itself - may be read and adapted: the patterns and the research are credited in CREDITS.md, every line carried over is named in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md, and the part is still built on TweaksV's shared code, as 2.0.0 rebuilt the five plugins, because a plugin merged as it is brings its own scanner, INI and log, what 1.x had. Code without a license, under all rights reserved or under FiveM's terms is idea only: none of it is read, and every game fact behind the part is found anew in the running Enhanced game with my tooling (a test build's dump, Ghidra; PATTERNS.md, "When Game Updates"). The original left is a Legacy plugin, and no pattern of a Legacy plugin carries over to Enhanced anyway (PATTERNS.md). So every part starts with a research pass in the dump that settles whether the limit is in Enhanced 1.0.1158.16 at all, where it lies, and whether it still binds - Enhanced may have raised it - and the part goes in only when a mod load hits it; a measurement of the limit under `DIAGNOSTIC_MODE`, as the box streamer and the decals have, comes first and says so.

## 2.7.0: SirenSettings - more sirens per vehicle and siren setting IDs past 255

The original: [SirenSetting Limit Adjuster](https://www.gta5-mods.com/scripts/sirensetting-limit-adjuster) 2.0.3 by cp702, 2020 to 2024-07-15, with a 2.0.4 for Legacy build 3788 in its repository, https://github.com/cpast/SirenSetting_Limit_Remover, pushed 2026-05-25. It raises the sirens a vehicle may have from 20 to 32 (`siren1` to `siren32`, `siren_glass` likewise; the 128 bones per vehicle stay), the siren setting ID of `carcols.meta` from 255 to 65535 (0, 255 and 65535 reserved; above 255 the base game's IDs clash with each other) and the number of unique siren settings, and writes a `SirenSettings.log` of every setting and vehicle loaded and every ID clash; its RagePluginHook integration means nothing on Enhanced, which has no RagePluginHook. Its license, checked 2026-10-26: none - the repository has no license file, and its README asks to link to the official upload instead of redistributing - so all rights reserved and idea only, none of its code read. What its author said of the technique on the CitizenFX forum in 2021: the siren setting ID is widened from 8 to 16 bits by turning a good number of byte references in the game's code into word references - many sites, every one to be found anew. Never ported to Enhanced by its author. Research: whether Enhanced keeps the limits of 255 IDs and 20 sirens, where the ID is stored and compared (the vehicle model's variation data, the array of siren settings), and what a car pack with its own `carcols.meta` reaches first; the plugin's file name for the conflict check, probably `SirenSetting_Limit_Adjuster.asi` after its solution. Third: emergency-vehicle packs for Enhanced bring their own siren settings, and 255 IDs run out with many add-ons.

The siren IDs, researched in the Enhanced dump on 2026-10-29 (the findings in my research tools' folder): Enhanced keeps the limit, at 8 bits on the whole load path - a siren setting's ID is a byte, in the setting and in the vehicle variation that names it, and a table of 256 bytes maps an ID to the setting's position, a byte too, in which 0 means no sirens: 256 IDs and 255 settings a vehicle can use (PATTERNS.md, "Siren IDs"). It binds silently: a pack's setting whose ID is in use is dropped, a clash within one file moves the later setting to the first free ID, and the parser cuts an ID above 255 in a file to its low byte (1534 becomes 254 - not 4, as a forum post has it), so packs that use large IDs clash with each other and with the game's own; a pack meets the IDs early, the 255 settings only with large collections. The plugin's file is `SirenSetting_Limit_Adjuster.asi` (the official 2.0.3 zip's, listed on 2026-10-29), a Legacy plugin, which the conflict check leaves out. The part's design: `[SIRENSETTINGS]` with a switch `LARGE_SIREN_IDS=1`, no size - the ID table moved on the relocation core to a fixed 65,536 entries, the loaders' 11 byte loads and stores of an ID made word ones and their 4 limits raised, the dedupe's loop rewritten, the 3 constructors' defaults, the two parser members' types made words - TweaksV's first write to the game's data - and the base loader's call redirected, so that the new table is filled whenever the game fills its own; the index stays a byte, so 255 settings stay the most. The limit is measured first (SPECIFICATIONS.md, section 11.14.1); its open question, what the game's PSO loader makes of a wider member in its binary files, is settled by the base game's numbers with the part off and on.

The research's result for the sirens per vehicle (2026-10-29, the dump of 1.0.1158.16): the limit is there and binds silently. The game lights 20 sirens, `siren1` to `siren20`, in four layers - a siren setting of 0x5E8 bytes holds 20 lights with their count and name behind them, a vehicle's siren state of 0x188 bytes keeps four arrays of 20, its renderer and six more loops run over 20 lights or bones, and the bones' hierarchy has IDs for `siren1` to `siren20` and their glass alone - and the 128 bones per vehicle stay, a bone's index being a signed byte. Packs make emergency vehicles with 20 sirens or with 32, nothing more; one made for 32 loads, and its lights 21 to 32 stay dark. A raise to 32 would take about 100 sites in three layers, the parser's member data in `.data`, which the dump leaves out, and the bones' table, whose allocation and fill were not found. So the limit is measured first (SPECIFICATIONS.md, section 11.14.2): with `DIAGNOSTIC_MODE=1` the measurement shows whether a mod load carries siren bones past `siren20`, and a raise - `SIRENS_PER_VEHICLE`, 20 to 32, 32 being the top the game's own 32-bit sets allow - is decided only after a boot shows such models, and needs a second research pass first.

My second test (2026-10-30) gave the boot D8 of PLAN.md asked for: three packs' 30- and 32-siren models - the CVPI LSPD pack's, the CHP FPIU and the LVMPD Crown Victoria - with 10 to 12 lights the game never lights, half the CHP FPIU's lightbar dark in the game; the siren IDs had room (33 of 255 settings, none colliding). SirenSettings becomes a phase of its own after 2.6.0, as 2.7.0, its second research pass first (PLAN.md, D14).

## 2.7.0: StaticBounds - a larger pool of static collision bounds

The original: [Static Bounds Limit Adjuster](https://www.gta5-mods.com/scripts/static-bound-limit-adjuster) v1.0.2 by Nochala (3_Washing_Machines), "[Legacy & Enhanced]", its source at https://github.com/Nochala/StaticboundsLimitAdjuster, commit 61507bc of 2026-10-09. It raises the game's `StaticBounds` pool (the hash of the name 0x2CDFE406, slots of 16 bytes), whose running out ends heavy map mods in an access violation - on my second test of 2.5.0 the game's crash reports showed the store at 10,406 of the 24,500 slots my `gameconfig.xml` gives it - to at least `STATICBOUNDS_SIZE`, 16,000 by default, at most 100,000, a larger `gameconfig.xml` value kept. Its `LICENSE.txt`, read on 2026-10-30, is the GPL-3.0's header with "Copyright (C) 2026 Nochala" and "either version 3 of the License, or (at your option) any later version": GPL-3.0-or-later, which TweaksV takes in, so its source may be read and adapted, its notice then in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md and its entry in CREDITS.md. Its plugin is `StaticBoundLimitAdjuster.asi` on its page and `StaticBoundsLimitAdjuster.asi` in its repository: both names for the conflict check.

I built the part for 2.6.0 on the wrong path and took it out before the tag (PLAN.md, D15; the part and its suite stand in the git history up to 66cd684). It redirected the size lookup's call found after a load of the pool's name, `BA 06 E4 DF 2C` - and my first proof boot of 2.6.0, on 2026-11-01, found that load nowhere in the running game. That call is only the original's fallback. Its way on Enhanced is two inline hooks made at its DLL attach (`PrepareEnhancedBootstrap` in its `CrashDiagnostics.cpp`, with their two patterns): one on the named pool's set-up, which notes the pool named `StaticBounds` with a stride of 16, one on the pool's allocator, which raises that pool's capacity at +0x18 to the key's value before the allocation and then takes both hooks off.

Phase C takes the part up anew through those two hooks, on the shared code. To settle when it is planned: whether both patterns match once in the running game; whether the game still makes the pool after TweaksV's `DllMain` - it makes its pools as the session starts; what the raise does with a larger `gameconfig.xml` value; and, by the never-silent rule, what says so on screen when a hook cannot be made or the pool is made without it. Its place in the order: phase C, beside SirenSettings.

## Phase C: the population's limits and the lighting capacity

Ideas from the page of [Resource Extensions [Enhanced]](https://www.gta5-mods.com/scripts/resource-extensions) 2.4 by Koukol, read on 2026-11-01: closed source and its redistribution forbidden, so the page's words alone, its plugin never read. Each needs a research pass in the dump, and a measurement before any raise.

- One pass on the population's limits: the vehicle model budget (its key `VehicleModelBudget`), the ped model budget (its "fewer repeated ped models"), the ped pool (256 to 768 on that page, 128 kept for missions) and the render queues of streamed peds and vehicles. Nothing is researched yet.
- The lighting capacity, a question of SirenSettings' research pass: many siren lights are where a load may reach it.
